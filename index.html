About Me

I’m a Taiwanese HCDE student who believes the best technology feels a lot like helping a friend—intuitive, thoughtful, and deeply empathetic.

Growing up with parents in the tech industry, I always looked up to them as my ultimate role models. They taught me early on that behind every complex system is a chance to make someone's life a little bit easier. Today, I channel that inspiration into crafting human-centered digital experiences, blending my lifelong love for technology with a genuine desire to help others.

But even the most analytical minds need a little grounding. Alongside my wireframes and prototypes, I have a deep appreciation for essential oils. In fact, you will almost always find me carrying a bit of rosemary—it is my personal symbol of perseverance, and a comforting reminder to stay focused and resilient through every design challenge.
